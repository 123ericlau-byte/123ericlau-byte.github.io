Hermes Local

Privacy policy

Effective November 8, 2026.

Data Hermes Local uses

When the operator connects a Google account, Hermes Local receives authorization tokens. Depending on the permissions the operator grants and the task being performed, it can access Drive file names and contents, Docs text, Sheets cell values, Calendar events, and Gmail messages, attachments, drafts, and labels.

How Hermes Local uses the data

Hermes Local uses Google data to carry out the operator's requests and routines. This can include finding or editing files, reading a planning sheet, updating calendar events, and drafting, organizing, or sending email. It does not sell Google data, use it for advertising, or use it to train general-purpose AI models.

Processing and sharing

This installation runs its language model locally. Task output can reach a destination the operator selects, such as a recipient of an email the operator asks Hermes to send. This branding site does not connect to the operator's Google account or receive its Workspace data.

Storage and deletion

Hermes Local stores Google authorization tokens under the operator's account on the Mac. Local conversation history, logs, and files created for tasks can retain information until the operator removes them. To end access, remove Hermes Local in Google Account connections. The operator can also delete local credentials and task records from the Mac, and manage copies created in Google services from the relevant service.

Google API data

Hermes Local's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the Hermes Local Google consent screen for questions about this policy or deletion.
